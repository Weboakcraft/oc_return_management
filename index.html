Every returned unit, accounted for.

Returns come back in mixed condition. This is where each unit is counted, inspected and followed until it is either back on the shelf, repaired, or rebuilt.

01 Return received
02 Inspected
03 OK, repair or damaged
04 Repair or production
05 Completed

Quantity is the unit of account throughout. Ten returns can hold twenty-seven units, and the difference matters.
